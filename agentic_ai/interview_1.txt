Question: what is agentic AI?
Answer: 
Agentic AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can independently plan, 
make decisions, and take actions to achieve a goal, rather than just responding 
to a single prompt.

Traditional AI (like a chatbot): You ask, "Summarize this report," and it gives you a summary.
Agentic AI: You ask, "Prepare a presentation on this report," and it can:
Read the report.
Identify the key points.
Search for supporting information if needed.
Create slides.
Revise them based on your feedback.
Deliver the final presentation.

The AI acts more like a digital assistant than a simple question-answering tool.

Key characteristics of Agentic AI
Goal-oriented: Works toward completing an objective.
Planning: Breaks complex tasks into smaller steps.
Decision-making: Chooses the best action based on available information.
Tool usage: Can use external tools such as web browsers, databases, calculators, APIs, or software applications.
Memory: Remembers relevant information during a task and, in some systems, across tasks.
Adaptation: Changes its approach if something doesn't work.

Generative AI is like a smart writer: it creates text, images, or code when asked.
Agentic AI is like a project manager: it decides what needs to be done, organizes the work, 
uses tools, and keeps working until the goal is achieved.

In short, agentic AI extends generative AI by adding planning, reasoning, memory, and the 
ability to take actions, making it capable of completing multi-step tasks with minimal 
human guidance.
